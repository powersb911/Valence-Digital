Common Questions

Answers before you ask

What makes Valence Digital different?

Most agencies focus on tools. We focus on behavior. Our founder holds a Master's in Applied Behavior Analysis — the science behind why people act and how to make the right actions feel natural. Your website removes friction so visitors call without confusion, your social content uses behavioral prompts that make engagement organic, and your growth systems are built around what actually reinforces repeat customer behavior.

What do I need to provide?

Very little. For social media, one or two real photos per week. For monthly reporting, access to your Google Analytics and Search Console. For the website build, a completed intake form. Everything else is managed on our end.

How is success measured?

Every month you receive a plain-language analytics report showing exactly what moved — search impressions, website visits, call clicks, review count, social reach, and lead volume. At the end of an engagement we review outcomes together with clear documentation.

Do I need a long-term contract?

No. Each engagement is a fixed-cost commitment with no obligation to renew. At the end you can continue, upgrade, or step away — no pressure and no penalty.

What if I already have a website?

The audit will tell us what's working and what isn't. If your existing site is structurally sound, we build on it. If it's holding you back, we'll recommend a rebuild as part of the Foundation tier — and explain exactly why in the debrief.
