Our Process

How We Deliver Results

Three deliberate steps. No long contracts, no mystery deliverables, no retainers that run forever.

01

Discovery Call

30 minutes, no cost, no obligation. You explain your business and goals. We listen for the gaps where your digital presence is leaking customers. You leave with a clear-eyed assessment of fit and cost.

02

Digital Audit & Debrief

We audit your website, search presence, social channels, and competitors. Every section gets a grade and written justification, followed by a prioritized action plan ranked by impact.

03

Take Action

Fixed cost, defined deliverables. You choose a tier that fits. At the end, renew, upgrade, take everything in-house, or walk away. No locked-in contracts.

04

Measure & Iterate

Monthly plain-language reports: search impressions, website visits, conversion rates, and demographics. At sprint end, we review the full arc and produce board-ready documentation.
